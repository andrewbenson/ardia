| 2026-10-28 | Presses on React Native views fall sideways into native views stacked under them. AppKit's default `-[NSView mouseDown:]` hands an unhandled press to `_nextResponderForEvent:`, which hit-tests the view's lower siblings before its superview, and RN never handles mouseDown in AppKit (its gesture recognizer does), so a press on an RN view over a native one also reaches the native one. This opened the command panel on a click in the RN top-strip peek; the toolbar's address field and buttons now act only on presses hit-tested to them (`NSView.isHitBy`), but other native views under RN overlays still take such presses | Medium, before more native views sit under RN overlays | The general fix: RN views pass an unhandled mouseDown to their superview only (`RCTView mouseDown:` in the react-native-macos patch), which reroutes every press in the RN UI and needs the full suite. `devWindow(n, "responders:x,y")` lists the chain a press at a point takes, with each sideways step |
